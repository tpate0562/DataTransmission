What is Dashcards?

Dashcards is a free, open-source flashcard website created by Tejas Patel and Miguel Diaz, based on Tejas's original C++ CLI program. Our design philosophy, and the namesake of the website, is speed: the ability to study quickly and efficiently without being bogged down by page loads and animations.

How the queue works

Every session puts its questions in a shuffled queue. Answer a question correctly on the first try and it's cleared. Miss it and it goes to the back of the queue; from then on it only clears once you answer it correctly two times in a row, and any miss sends it back again with the count reset. The session ends when the queue is empty. At the end you get a list of everything you missed, most-missed first, and can drill just those again.

Progress is saved in your browser after every answer, so you can close the tab and resume later.

How to Use

Pick a deck on the home page. The PSTAT 100 midterm practice test lets you choose which topics to include and how many questions to draw. To use your own cards, create a deck in Create Deck with a minimum of 5 terms, download it, and open it with Load File on the home page.

Keys: 1–4 or A–D answer, Enter goes to the next question.

A Note on Presets

Besides the PSTAT 100 practice test, the presets are the vocab lists for Mr. Coursey's AP English classes, plus a few miscellaneous decks. If you would like a new preset to be added, please contact us or submit a pull request!
